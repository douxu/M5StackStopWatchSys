# Weather location data

The bundled province/city snapshot is derived from the Ministry of Civil Affairs'
**2024 年中华人民共和国县以上行政区划代码**, published in 2025:
https://www.mca.gov.cn/mzsj/xzqh/2025/202401xzqh.html

`regions.json` preserves the extracted two-level dataset. `regions.cpp` is the
firmware representation. Both are generated by:

```sh
python3 scripts/generate_weather_regions.py /path/to/202401xzqh.html
```

- 34 province-level entries and 380 second-level weather choices.
- Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai and Chongqing each have exactly one matching city.
- Prefecture-level cities, autonomous prefectures, leagues and regions are kept.
- Directly administered county-level locations with no prefecture parent are
  placed at the second level so those areas remain selectable; no third level.
- Hong Kong and Macao each have one weather location.
- The MCA table contains only the Taiwan province entry. Nine city names are
  supplemented from the China government overview:
  https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2020-07/28/content_5530577.htm
  Their IDs 9000001–9000009 are application identifiers, **not** MCA division codes.
- Source footnote asterisks are removed from names.
- This is a versioned snapshot, not a claim of live 2026 administrative boundaries.

Queries use the selected city and province names. wttr.in may return a nearby
weather reporting location; the UI retains the selected city label. Automatic
IP location is saved as the provider's coordinates and display name. Coordinates
are not presented as GPS measurements.

## Chinese font

`weather_city_24.c` is a 24px, 2bpp, uncompressed subset of Noto Sans CJK SC Regular
(https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk), licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; the license is
in `main/assets/fonts/Noto-OFL.txt`. The subset is named `weather_city_24` and
contains ASCII plus characters in `region_glyphs.txt`. Uncompressed output is
required because this firmware disables `LV_USE_FONT_COMPRESSED`.

To regenerate with the `lv_font_conv` CLI, pass `--size 24 --bpp 2 --format lvgl`,
`--font /path/to/NotoSansCJKsc-Regular.otf`, `-r 0x20-0x7E`, `--symbols` with the
contents of `region_glyphs.txt`, `--no-compress --no-kerning`, and
`--lv-font-name weather_city_24 -o main/assets/fonts/weather_city_24.c`.
